INDEPENDENT PRODUCT PROPOSAL

03 / QUESTARENA

Prove the core multiplayer loop before expanding the feature set.

QuestArena VR is a fictional product scenario from an assignment brief. The proposed mode lets players compete in timed movement challenges with friends or matched players.

Product hypothesis: Reliable, fair and easy-to-start multiplayer may add social motivation and encourage repeat play. The supplied brief does not establish that this need has been validated.

Players and journey

Initial user groups to validate include social players who want to challenge friends, competitive players who value fair scoring, and progress-oriented players who want results saved over time. These are working hypotheses, not research findings.

Open mode → invite / match → join lobby → timed challenge → view result → save progress → play again.

MVP recommendation

  • Premium access validation
  • Friend invite or basic matchmaking
  • Lobby and timed challenge flow
  • Consistent score calculation
  • Result display and reliable persistence
  • Essential disconnect and error handling
  • Telemetry for the core journey

Defer advanced tournaments, elaborate social features and richer leaderboard capabilities until the core experience is proven.

Trust is part of the feature

  • Find a match: Lobby creation and joining communicate waiting, success or failure; players can cancel or retry.
  • Play fairly: Rules and timer are visible before play; scoring is consistent and results are understandable.
  • Keep the result: Completed results persist and can be retrieved; save failures are surfaced and investigated.
  • Recover gracefully: Connection drops follow a defined resume, completion or cancellation path; duplicate result submissions are prevented.

Monetisation and rollout

The brief indicates premium-gated access but does not specify the existing plan or price. I would first test the mode within the current premium offer rather than introduce a separate charge without willingness-to-pay evidence. A staged approach would move from an instrumented beta to limited premium rollout, then broader availability only after reliability and value gates are met.

Metrics

Measure mode discovery and activation, matchmaking success and time, match completion, result-save success, disconnect recovery, repeat play and premium conversion. Compare against a credible baseline before attributing retention or revenue impact to the feature.

What this work demonstrates

Product framing, end-to-end journey design, user stories and acceptance criteria, MVP trade-offs, reliability thinking, telemetry, staged rollout and hypothesis-led monetisation.

Status and evidence: Independent proposal based on a fictional assignment brief. No shipped feature, validated user research, commercial results or live rollout are claimed. Demand, pricing, costs and delivery estimates remain assumptions to validate.
